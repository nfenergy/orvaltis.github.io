About ORVALTIS

A boutique built on relevance.

Independent origination, counterparty engagement and a commitment to confidentiality define the model we intend to build.

01

The experience behind the initiative

ORVALTIS is being shaped to bring coherence to work already under way: sourcing energy opportunities, engaging with developers and landowners, and reviewing the investment criteria of industrial and institutional counterparties. Prior activity belongs to the promoters and their professional background, not to a longstanding ORVALTIS entity.

02

Our mission

To identify opportunities with intelligible industrial fundamentals, qualify their principal characteristics and establish focused dialogue between developers, project owners and potential counterparties. We believe lasting professional relationships depend on sound information and clear commitments.

03

Work in progress

The promoters’ existing work includes a review of solar, agrivoltaic and BESS initiatives, dialogue with Italian and international industrial and financial parties, and requests for additional information on selected opportunities. These are documented preparatory activities and discussions—not completed mandates or closed transactions.

04

A long-term perspective

Our immediate ambition is to establish a specialist commercial advisory and origination platform. Any future principal investment activity would be separate and subject to its own structure, resources and applicable regulatory framework.

We do not seek to add noise to the market. We aim to improve the quality of the conversations.

01 / Our positioning

ORVALTIS brings together experience and market relationships developed by its promoters, with a focus on origination and commercial introductions. Our purpose is not to manage capital, but to make opportunities easier to assess.
