Our approach

Trust is built
before the transaction.

A disciplined process from initial sourcing to organised commercial dialogue.

01

Origination

Direct identification of opportunities and owners; initial documentation gathered within the agreed scope.

02

Qualification

Preliminary review of location, stage of development, grid position, transaction perimeter and owner availability.

03

Matching

Identification of relevant counterparties against actual criteria, rather than indiscriminate circulation.

04

Disclosure

Controlled release of information in line with confidentiality arrangements and the owner’s consent.

05

Dialogue

Support for the commercial conversation; technical, legal and financial reviews are undertaken by the relevant appointed professionals.

Confidentiality means disciplined information flow—not ambiguity about project fundamentals.
